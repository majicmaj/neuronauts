# VS energy VFX credits

The VS energy meter uses two artist-made, non-generative asset packs. Both are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); attribution is not required, but the artists are credited here intentionally.

## Wenrexa — Assets Free Laser Bullets Pack 2020

- Source: https://wenrexa.itch.io/laser2020
- Shipped file: `wenrexa-laser-red.png` (pack sprite `64.png`)
- Use: opposing laser textures at the energy collision. The blue-side version is mirrored and color-shifted in CSS.
- License: Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal
- Artist page states that no generative AI was used.

## Luis Zuno / Ansimuz — Warped Shooting Fx

- Source: https://ansimuz.itch.io/warped-shooting-fx
- Shipped files: `luis-zuno-charged.gif` (the pack’s charged-effect preview) and `luis-zuno-charged-still.png` (a reduced-motion frame extracted from that animation)
- Use: animated collision burst and its static reduced-motion alternative
- License: Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal; the downloaded pack also includes `public-license.pdf` naming Luis Zuno as author.
- Artist page states that no generative AI was used.
